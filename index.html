Discover California's Beauty

California offers a breathtaking landscape where dramatic coastal cliffs crash into the Pacific Ocean along the iconic Pacific Coast Highway. Rugged mountains like the Sierra Nevada provide a majestic backdrop to an active, outdoor lifestyle, seamlessly blending pristine wilderness with a high quality of life. From sun-drenched beaches to misty redwood forests, the California experience is defined by this unparalleled natural beauty, inspiring a culture centered on wellness, coastal exploration, and open-air adventure.

Amazing Facts About California!

  • California contains both Mount Whitney, the highest peak in the contiguous U.S., and Death Valley, the lowest and hottest place in North America, separated by less than 90 miles.
  • Sequoia National Park is home to General Sherman, a giant sequoia that is the largest tree on Earth by volume.
  • The very first ARPANET message—the foundation of today's internet—was transmitted from UCLA to the Stanford Research Institute in 1969.
  • The grizzly bear featured on the official state flag is actually extinct in the wild within California today.
  • California produces approximately 80% of the entire world's almond supply.
  • California is the only U.S. state to have hosted both the Summer (1932, 1984) and Winter (1960) Olympic Games.
  • The state experiences roughly 10,000 earthquakes every single year, though most are too small to feel.
  • Walt Disney opened his very first theme park in Anaheim, California, in the summer of 1955.
  • The name "California" originates from a 1510 Spanish romance novel that described a mythical island ruled by a queen named Calafia.
  • The town of Fallbrook, California, is known as the "Avocado Capital of the World" and hosts an annual festival celebrating the fruit.
